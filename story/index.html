Our story

Six brothers. One kitchen.

Brothers Pizza Company has served Crawfordsville since September 4, 2013, from the corner of Market Street and Green Street.

The Lowe brothers

01
01

Pizza and a movie

The brothers grew up on family “pizza and a movie” nights at home. That became the mission and the motto: Pizza for your family. They want other Crawfordsville families to have their own pizza nights.

02

A second run at it

They had been in the pizza business before. They stepped back to rework their plan and their recipes, then learned pizza-making in a professional kitchen alongside a master baker from Godfather’s Pizza.

03

Serious about ingredients

Their tomatoes come from Stanislaus, a family-run company for 80 years that also trained them on the sauce. Their cheeses come through Delco, an Italian specialty food supplier.

04

Someone has to break the tie

Six brothers means six opinions. Mark’s advice to other family businesses is to set boundaries early and put one person in charge. Every Saturday after close, the whole family gets together for game night with pizza and dessert. The brothers have even been writing their own tabletop game, Garden Fall.

Giving back

Christmas on Green St.

Since 2013 the brothers have hosted Santa on Green Street every December. Kids meet him, get a photo and pick out a toy of their own. Some years there’s been a sleigh and a reindeer to feed. It started with five people. Now the line goes around the building.

Each year Santa also hands out two golden jingle bells, like the one in The Polar Express, to two children he thinks need it most. Some parents have said the toy their child chose would be their only gift that Christmas.
