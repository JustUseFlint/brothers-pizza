Six brothers, one kitchen

Luke, Jack, John, Michael, Mark and Brandon.

The Lowe brothers grew up on “pizza and a movie” nights. In 2013 they opened at the corner of Market and Green to give other families the same thing. They learned the craft from a master baker from Godfather’s Pizza, make their sauce from Stanislaus tomatoes and buy their cheeses through Delco.

Read our story
01

The sauce

Fresh-packed tomatoes from Stanislaus, a family business for 80 years. Stanislaus also trained the brothers on the recipe.

02

The cheese

Sourced through Delco, an Italian specialty food supplier. The Cheese Boss and Boss Bites put it front and centre.

03

The dough

Hand tossed every time, and the same dough makes the breadsticks. If you visit, you might catch Jack spinning dough overhead.

Christmas on Green St.

Five people came the first year. Now the line wraps the building.

Every December since 2013 the brothers host Santa on Green Street, and every child picks out a toy. They also give away turkeys at Thanksgiving and host fundraisers for schools, teams and families who need a hand.
